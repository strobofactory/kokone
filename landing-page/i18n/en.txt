Before you begin,
a few things to know.
Do I need to write a long entry every day?
No long entries needed. Say a few words, choose a photo, or use whichever method feels right that day.
Can I use my handwritten journal?
Photograph handwritten entries or notes to extract the text and save it. Results depend on handwriting and photo quality, so please review the extracted text.
What does AI analysis show?
It presents emotional patterns in your entry as percentages, along with reflection messages and prompts. Results are a guide for reflection, not a medical diagnosis.
Where is my diary stored?
Diary entries are stored on your device. With your consent, AI analysis sends entry content to an external AI service. You can change this permission in settings. See the privacy policy for details.
